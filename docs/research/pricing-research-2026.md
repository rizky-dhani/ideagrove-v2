## 1. Headline: There Is No Single "Market Price"

Indonesian web pricing spans **Rp 300K → Rp 1 miliar+**. That range is not noise — it is **four distinct vendor segments** that are not competing with each other:

| Segment | Price band | Who buys | What they get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Instant template / drag-drop** | Rp 300K – 1,5jt | Micro-UMKM, warung, personal | Pre-made theme, logo/color swap, 3–5 pages, 3–5 days |
| **Freelancer / small studio** | Rp 1jt – 15jt | Small business, startup | Custom-ish WP/Elementor, 2–4 weeks |
| **Local agency** | Rp 5jt – 25jt (profile) / 25jt – 100jt (complex) | Established SMB, hospitality | Team (PM/UI/UX/dev/SEO), structured process |
| **Enterprise / custom app** | Rp 100jt – 1 miliar+ | Corporate, e-commerce, govt | Full custom stack, integrations, 2–6 months |

**Key insight:** you do not have a "price". You have a **segment**. Pick the segment by *who you serve*, then price inside it.

## 3. The Price-Sensitive Buyer — What Actually Happens

Price sensitivity in Indonesia is **not** "wants cheapest". It is:

1. **Anchored on the Rp 300K–1,5jt template tier.** Every prospect has seen a Rp 500K "website" ad. Your job is to **re-anchor** before quoting — never quote into their anchor.
2. **Lump-sum psychology, not subscription.** IDR cash flow favors one-time payment. Monthly retainers need a *reason* (maintenance, SEO) — and even then, annual prepay with discount sells better than monthly.
3. **Trust via WhatsApp, not forms.** Price transparency on-site reduces friction, but the close happens in chat. Show packages publicly, then converse.
4. **Cash-flow-sensitive, not value-insensitive.** The same buyer who flinches at Rp 20jt lump sum will accept Rp 8jt + Rp 1,5jt/mo. **Payment structure is a pricing lever.**
5. **"Mahal" is usually scope confusion, not price objection.** They compare a Rp 500K template to a Rp 10jt custom site and see no difference *until you show the difference* (speed, mobile, SEO, bookings, ownership).

### The OTA hook (hospitality-specific, strongest value argument)

Hotels/villas lose **15–20% commission** to OTAs (Booking/Agoda/Airbnb). A direct-booking site that shifts even a handful of bookings/month pays for itself. **Price against the commission saved, not against the template price.**

## 5. Price-Sensitive Playbook (how to quote without racing to the bottom)

1. **Publish prices.** Transparency builds trust in this market and pre-qualifies. (Also makes you AI-citable — see `ai-seo`/`schema` skills.)
2. **Anchor with Tier 3 first** in the visual order (best-on-left or highlighted), sell Tier 2.
3. **Lead with the OTA commission math** for hospitality — reframes from "cost" to "savings".
4. **Offer 3 payment structures** for any quote ≥ Rp 10jt: 50/50 · 3 milestones · 12-month installment at small markup.
5. **Never discount — reduce scope.** "Rp 8jt" beats "Rp 12jt, 33% off". Discounting destroys the anchor permanently.
6. **Separate build from care.** One-time build gets you in; monthly care is where margin lives and where churn is preventable.
7. **Land-and-expand:** start with a paid audit/optimization (Rp 1,5jt – 3jt) for prospects who balk at build price. Converts skeptics into clients.
8. **Don't fight the Rp 500K tier.** Say it plainly: "those are templates; you own nothing, it's slow, and it won't rank." Then show a real before/after.

## 6. Sanity Checks & Risks

- **Renewal cliff:** year-1 hosting included means year-2 renewal (Rp 790K – 1,5jt) triggers churn. Price hosting honestly or bundle into Care plans.
- **Scope creep** is the #1 margin killer in this market. Fixed-price requires a hard scope doc.
- **Vendor pages overstate.** Rp 15jt–40jt claims for company profiles exist (Sagara Ruang) alongside Rp 1jt claims. Verify against your own close rate, not vendor blogs.
- **Bali is dual-market:** local IDR clients (price-sensitive) + foreign-owned villas (pay in USD/AUD, price-insensitive). **Same deliverable, two price lists.** Consider an international-facing tier priced in USD.
- **SEO retainer range is huge** (Rp 3jt–30jt/mo) — if you enter this, define deliverables or you'll be compared on price alone.
